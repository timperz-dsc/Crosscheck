### Breakers the probe may touch

All 356 `E_CB_*` stay in the profile. Only these 11 are marked probeable (spread over the panels): `E_CB_105VU_A01`, `E_CB_106VU_B03`, `E_CB_121VU_M19`, `E_CB_121VU_P20`, `E_CB_122VU_W02`, `E_CB_122VU_Y06`, `E_CB_123VU_AB09`, `E_CB_2000VU_J06`, `E_CB_2001VU_A03`, `E_CB_49VU_D02`, `E_CB_49VU_G02`.

Breaker names are only panel and grid position, so the scan cannot say which circuit each one protects. Step 2 probes them on the ground, powered, and restores each immediately.

## Surprises and things to check

- 101 switches got `values [0, 1]` from their name and a scan value of 0 or 1. The scan has one value per var, so a 3-position switch with a binary-looking name would be limited wrongly. Step 2 must confirm each one; the note on the entry says so.
- All 356 `E_CB_*` read 1, and the `E_` prefix is the encoder prefix, so they might be counters rather than 0/1 breakers. They are `verified [0, 1]` with the note "semantics unverified", as asked.
- 97 switches are on the review list because the position count is unknown (scan value 2 or more, or a multi-position name). `S_FCU_EFIS2_ND_MODE` and `ND_ZOOM` are probably like EFIS1 but are not proven, so they are there too.
- Many `S_*_Anim` vars read 2 (for example `S_OH_ELEC_GEN1_Anim`): the animation has three states for what looks like a two-position switch. They are `ignore`.
- 365 switches are on the review list because momentary or latching cannot be told from the scan: most FCU/EFIS, MIP and flight-control pushbuttons, the audio panels, the RMPs, `_BUTTON`/`_PUSH`/`_UP`/`_DOWN` names. For example the fire buttons are not guessed as pulses. Writing a momentary button as an absolute value would leave it stuck, so these need the step 2 probe.
- `*GUARD*` matches nothing in this scan (guards are named `*_Cover`); it stays in the deny list as a catch-all.
- `match.titles` in the aircraft section is still a TODO (needs the title the sim reports).
